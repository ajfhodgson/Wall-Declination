Wall Declination Calculator

Using the Declination Measuring Tool, record dates, times and angles when the sun's rays pass directly down the channel. Enter the dates, times and measured angles below.

This web app calculates the wall's Declination east of south by averaging the measurements.

Outlier measurements can be removed from the calculation by clearing the check-box against that measurement.

The calculated solar azimuth is calculated using Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, see http://www.jgiesen.de/sunpos/month.html
